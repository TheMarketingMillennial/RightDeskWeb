Delete Your RightDesk Reports Account

You may request deletion of your RightDesk Reports account and the data associated with your account at any time.

To request account deletion

Submit the account deletion form below using the email address associated with your RightDesk Reports account. We may contact you to verify your identity before processing the request. You can also email privacy@rightdeskreports.com from the address on your account.

What will be deleted

When your account is deleted, your RightDesk account and associated personal data will be deleted, including your workplace incident reports and other information associated with your account, except information that RightDesk is legally required or permitted to retain.

Important Account deletion is permanent. Once your documentation has been deleted, it cannot be recovered. If you have an active subscription, follow the applicable subscription cancellation instructions to prevent future billing.
