RightDesk Reports Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

RightDesk Reports (“RightDesk,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is operated by The Marketing Millennial.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share information when you use the RightDesk Reports Android application, our web application at https://app.rightdeskreports.com, or our website at https://rightdeskreports.com.

We recognize that workplace incidents can involve sensitive personal and professional information. Protecting that information and being transparent about how it is handled are important parts of our service.

For privacy questions, contact privacy@rightdeskreports.com.

Our Commitment to Your Privacy
  • Your workplace incidents, notes, attachments, and saved reports are stored in your account using Google Firebase cloud services.
  • We do not sell users' private workplace documentation.
  • We do not use incident descriptions or saved reports for advertising.
  • Other ordinary users cannot access someone's private incident records.
  • Analytics events do not include incident content.
  • AI report generation is optional and is initiated by the user.
  • When you request an AI-generated report, the selected information is securely transmitted to our server and OpenAI for processing.
  • Sponsored access codes do not provide sponsoring organizations with access to users' incident records.
  • RightDesk Reports uses encryption in transit and at rest but does not claim to provide end-to-end encryption.
  • You can delete individual records and request deletion of your account and associated information.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication information.

Authentication is managed through Google Firebase Authentication. We do not receive or store your password in readable form.

Depending on the information you choose to provide, your account may also contain employment-related details, including your employer's name, department, employee identification number, or other profile information.

Workplace incident records

RightDesk Reports allows you to document workplace experiences and maintain an organized record of events.

Information you voluntarily enter may include:

  • Incident descriptions, dates, times, and locations
  • Names and roles of individuals involved
  • Witness information
  • Statements or conversations
  • Workplace policies or procedures
  • Correspondence with management or human resources
  • Actions taken and responses received
  • Personal observations and notes
  • The impact of an incident
  • Follow-up communications and outcomes

You determine what information to include in your records.

Photos and attachments

You may choose to upload photos, screenshots, or supporting files.

We collect only the attachments you select and submit through the application.

Saved reports

When you generate and save a report, the resulting document may be stored in your account for future access.

Voice dictation

If you use voice dictation, speech recognition technology converts spoken words into text. The resulting text is handled as part of the information you choose to enter into the application.

Attorney connection requests

If you choose to request assistance connecting with an attorney, we may collect your name, contact information, ZIP code, general case category, notes you provide, and preferred method of communication.

We may also collect relevant request details needed to process your inquiry.

Submitting an attorney connection request is voluntary.

Promotional and sponsored access codes

If you redeem a promotional, sponsored, or firm-provided access code, we record information necessary to validate the code and apply the appropriate account access.

Redeeming an access code does not automatically give the sponsoring organization access to your incident records, attachments, or saved reports.

Communications

We collect information you voluntarily submit when contacting customer support, requesting assistance, asking privacy-related questions, or requesting account deletion.

Information Collected Automatically

Application and website analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Google Analytics 4 to understand how people interact with RightDesk Reports.

Analytics may include website visits, application launches, screen views, device information, traffic sources, and general interactions.

Our Android application does not request the Android advertising ID permission. However, analytics services may use other technical identifiers, such as app instance identifiers, to measure application usage.

Our website may use cookies and other technologies to measure website traffic and the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.

We do not include workplace incident descriptions, saved reports, attachments, or other private workplace documentation in analytics events.

Technical information

Our application, website, hosting providers, and security systems may automatically process information such as IP addresses, device information, request timestamps, error messages, and server activity.

This information helps us maintain functionality, diagnose problems, and protect the service.

We do not intentionally include the contents of private incident records in technical logs.

Aggregate incident information

RightDesk Reports may collect limited structured information about documented incidents for internal analysis.

This may include incident categories, general severity classifications, reporting periods, account plan types, and application platforms.

We use this information to better understand workplace trends and improve our services.

Aggregate reporting is intended to identify broader patterns rather than individual employees or specific incidents.

We do not include private incident narratives or identifying employee details in reports presented as anonymous or aggregate statistics.

Information We Do Not Intentionally Access

RightDesk Reports does not require access to your device contacts or continuous precise-location tracking to provide its core documentation features.

We do not access your entire photo library. Photos and attachments are collected when you choose to provide them.

Certain third-party services may process device identifiers, IP addresses, or approximate location information as described in this policy.

2. Payments and Subscriptions

RightDesk Reports offers subscription-based services.

Payments made through the Android application are processed through Google Play Billing. Payments made through our web application are processed through Stripe.

Google and Stripe handle payment processing under their respective privacy and security practices.

We do not receive or store your complete payment card number.

We may receive information necessary to manage your subscription, including:

  • Purchase confirmation
  • Subscription status
  • Subscription plan
  • Transaction or subscription identifiers
  • Renewal, cancellation, or expiration status
  • Payment-related account identifiers

For Stripe payments, we may provide your email address and account identifier to associate the transaction with your RightDesk Reports account.

Payment information is used to provide purchased services, manage subscription access, respond to billing inquiries, and maintain required business records.

3. Where Your Information Is Stored and How It Is Protected

Cloud Storage

RightDesk Reports uses Google Firebase and Google Cloud services to store and manage account information and application data.

Workplace incidents and saved reports are stored using Cloud Firestore. Attachments are stored using Firebase Storage.

These services provide encryption in transit and at rest.

Application security rules and authentication controls are intended to restrict access to authorized users and prevent ordinary users from viewing records belonging to other accounts.

Account Security

Your account is protected by the authentication methods available through Firebase Authentication.

We encourage you to use a strong password, protect access to your email account, and secure the devices you use to access RightDesk Reports.

Anyone who gains unauthorized access to your account or an unlocked device may be able to view information available within that session.

Authorized Administrative Access

RightDesk Reports is not an end-to-end encrypted service.

Although information is encrypted during transmission and storage, authorized personnel with appropriate administrative access may be technically capable of accessing information maintained within our systems.

We limit administrative access to legitimate operational, security, support, and legal purposes.

Information Stored on Your Device

The application may temporarily store information or cached data on your device to support functionality and improve performance.

Protecting your device and account credentials helps reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

Security Limitations

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information.

However, no online service, cloud storage system, or electronic transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of a security incident affecting personal information, we will evaluate our notification obligations and provide applicable notices as required by law.

4. AI-Generated Reports and Third-Party Service Providers

AI Report Generation

RightDesk Reports provides an optional feature that helps users organize their documented workplace incidents into written reports.

When you request an AI-generated report, the information selected for that report is transmitted over an encrypted connection to our application server hosted by Railway.

Our server then sends the information necessary to generate the report to OpenAI.

Depending on your selections, this information may include incident descriptions, dates, names, workplace details, correspondence, and other information you entered.

OpenAI processes the submitted information and returns generated report text.

The completed report is then returned to RightDesk Reports, where it may be saved to your account.

Important Information About AI Processing

  • AI report generation is optional.
  • Incident content is submitted to OpenAI when you request report generation, not merely because you created an incident record.
  • The information submitted may contain sensitive personal or workplace details.
  • AI-generated reports are based on the information you provide and may contain errors or omissions.
  • You should review reports for accuracy before sharing or relying on them.
  • AI-generated reports do not constitute legal advice or establish that a legal violation occurred.

When a user requests an AI-generated report, the selected incident information is securely transmitted to our Railway-hosted server and forwarded to OpenAI for report generation.

Our application server does not retain a separate permanent copy of the incident narratives submitted for report generation.

Operational logs may record information such as the number of incidents processed and the report type, but do not contain the text of the incidents.

Generated reports are returned to the application and may be saved to the user's Firebase account.

OpenAI states that information submitted through its API is not used to train its models by default, unless the customer opts into data sharing for that purpose. OpenAI may retain certain information for abuse monitoring or service operation according to its applicable policies and API configuration.

For more information, visit https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/.

Voice Dictation

Voice dictation uses speech recognition technology available through your device or browser.

Depending on your device and settings, audio may be processed by Google, Apple, or another speech recognition provider.

RightDesk Reports does not intentionally store recordings of your dictated audio as part of its incident documentation service.

We receive and process the resulting text that you choose to enter or save.

You can disable microphone permissions through your device settings.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Keeping Your Information

We generally retain account information, workplace records, attachments, and saved reports while your account remains active, unless you delete the information or request account deletion.

Certain technical, transaction, and operational records may be maintained for shorter or longer periods based on service requirements, security needs, or legal obligations.

Deleting Individual Records

You may delete individual incident records and saved reports using available application features.

Associated files or copies may remain temporarily in provider backups or other systems until applicable retention or deletion processes are completed.

Requesting Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their RightDesk Reports account and associated personal information by submitting a request at:

https://rightdeskreports.com/delete-account/

Users may also email privacy@rightdeskreports.com.

Account deletion requests are reviewed and processed manually. We may request reasonable identity verification before processing a request.

Once a request is verified, we will delete the user's Firebase Authentication account, associated Firestore records, saved reports, and uploaded photos and attachments from our active application systems, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate retention exceptions.

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days.

Certain information may remain temporarily in routine backups or be retained where required or permitted by law.

Deleting the application from a device does not automatically delete the user's account or cloud-stored information.

Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Users must cancel subscriptions through the applicable payment provider to prevent future charges.

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may remain after account deletion when retention is necessary or permitted, including:

  • Financial or subscription records required for accounting or legal compliance
  • Information necessary to resolve disputes or prevent fraud
  • Records subject to lawful preservation requirements
  • Security or operational records maintained for legitimate purposes
  • Data contained in routine backups until those backups expire or are overwritten
  • Aggregate or de-identified statistical information that can no longer reasonably identify an individual
