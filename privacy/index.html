Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

RightDesk Reports (“RightDesk”, “we”, “us”) is operated by The Marketing Millennial. This policy explains what information the RightDesk Reports Android app, the web app at app.rightdeskreports.com, and the website at rightdeskreports.com collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. Questions about this policy can go to privacy@rightdeskreports.com.

The short version
  • Your incidents, notes, photos and saved reports are stored in your account in Google’s cloud (Firebase), not only on your device.
  • Your information is encrypted in transit and at rest, and other users cannot see it. It is not end-to-end encrypted (see section 3).
  • When you ask for a report, the incident details you selected are sent to our server and on to OpenAI, which writes the report text.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can delete individual records in the app, or ask us to delete your whole account at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password in readable form). Optional details such as employee ID, department and company name, if you enter them.
  • Incident content: everything you record about an incident, such as the description, date, time, location, the people involved and their roles, witnesses and any contact details you enter for them, exact words used, policies you believe were violated, the impact on you, whether and to whom you reported it, how HR or management responded, correspondence log entries, and notes.
  • Photos and files: only the photos or files you choose to attach to an incident.
  • Saved reports: the report text generated for you, which is saved to your account.
  • Voice dictation: if you use the microphone to dictate into a form, your device’s speech recognition converts it to text. See section 4.
  • Attorney connection requests (optional): if you ask us to connect you with an attorney, we collect your name, email, optional phone number, ZIP code, type of case, any notes you add, your plan, the report type, your device platform and your preferred contact method.
  • Access codes: if you redeem a sponsor or firm access code, we record which code you used so your plan can be applied.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to our support or privacy addresses, and account deletion requests submitted through the website.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage analytics: we use Google Analytics for Firebase (Android) and Google Analytics 4 (web) to count anonymous events such as screens viewed, sign-ups and reports generated. These events do not include your name, email, or any incident content.
  • Aggregate incident statistics: when you log an incident, we record the category, severity, month, your plan type and your platform. This record does not include any text you wrote and is not linked to your name or email.
  • Technical data: basic device and app information (such as app version and platform) and server logs needed to run and secure the service. We do not intentionally log the content of your incidents.
  • Website: our website uses Google Analytics and loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details when you visit. Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser; you can block or clear cookies in your browser settings.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or your device advertising ID. We do not read photos on your device other than the ones you attach.

2. Payments

Paid plans are purchased through Google Play on Android, or through Stripe on the web. Card and payment details are handled by Google or Stripe; we never receive your full card number. We receive confirmation of your purchase and subscription status, and when you pay through Stripe we send your email address and account ID to Stripe so the payment can be matched to your account.

3. Where your information is stored and how it is protected

  • Cloud storage: your account, incidents and saved reports are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, and your attached photos in Firebase Storage, both operated by Google. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Security rules limit access so that signed-in users can read and write only their own records.
  • On your device: the app may keep a copy of some of your data on your device so it loads quickly. Anyone who can unlock your device or sign in to your account may be able to see it, so please protect both.
  • Access by us: your data is encrypted, but it is not end-to-end encrypted. A small number of authorized people can access the systems that hold it, and we limit that access to what is needed to operate, secure and support the service and to meet legal obligations.
  • Connections: all communication between the app, our server and our service providers uses HTTPS/TLS.

4. Report writing and other service providers

When you tap to generate a report, the incident details you selected, together with the report type, tone, and any name, employee ID, department, manager and company details you entered, are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which is hosted on Railway. The server passes them to OpenAI, which writes the report text. The text is returned to your account and saved there.

  • We do not store your incident content on our server after the report is generated.
  • According to OpenAI’s published policy, information sent through its business interface is not used to train its models by default, and may be retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse. See openai.com/enterprise-privacy.
  • Generating a report is always your choice. Nothing is sent until you request it.
  • Reports are drafts based on what you entered. Please review them for accuracy before you share them.

We use the following service providers to run RightDesk. Each processes information only to provide its service to us:

  • Google (Firebase, Google Cloud, Google Play, Google Analytics, Google Fonts): sign-in, database, file storage, purchases, analytics. Privacy policy
  • OpenAI: writing the report text you request. Privacy policy
  • Railway: hosting our report server. Privacy policy
  • Stripe: web payments. Privacy policy
  • Netlify: hosting our website and web app and receiving website form submissions (such as deletion requests and attorney partner enquiries). Privacy policy

Voice dictation. The microphone is used only when you tap the dictation button. Speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service built into your device (provided by Google on Android, or by Apple on iPhone and iPad), which may process audio on its own servers under its own privacy policy. We do not receive, record or store your audio, only the resulting text that you choose to keep. You can turn off microphone access in your device settings; the app works without it.

5. How we use and share your information

We use your information to provide the service you asked for (storing your records, generating your reports, applying your plan), to keep it secure, to answer your requests, to improve how the app works using anonymous statistics, and to meet legal obligations.

  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • Attorneys: we share your details with an attorney only if you submit an attorney connection request, and only for the purpose of that request.
  • Sponsor or firm codes: redeeming a code applies a plan to your account. It does not give the sponsoring organization access to your incidents.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and service.
  • Business changes: if RightDesk is ever merged with or acquired by another company, we will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6. Sensitive information

Incident records can contain sensitive details, such as descriptions of harassment or discrimination and information about other people, including colleagues and managers. You decide what to enter. Please enter other people’s contact details only if you are comfortable doing so. We treat this information with care, use it only as described in this policy, and send it to a service provider only when you ask us to generate a report.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

  • In the app: you can delete individual incidents and saved reports at any time.
  • Your whole account: submit the form at rightdeskreports.com/delete-account, or email privacy@rightdeskreports.com from the address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days. This removes your account, incidents, saved reports and attached photos.
  • Deleting the app does not delete your account or your cloud data. You need to request account deletion as described above.
  • What may remain: information we are legally required or permitted to keep (for example purchase records held by Google or Stripe), copies in routine backups until they expire, and anonymous statistics that cannot be linked to you.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Please cancel it in Google Play or through your Stripe receipt to avoid future charges.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA, UK and some US states such as California), you may have the right to access the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a portable format, object to or restrict certain uses, and withdraw consent. You can edit your records and export reports as PDF in the app. For anything else, email privacy@rightdeskreports.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK and are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

9. International transfers

RightDesk and its service providers are based in or operate from the United States, so your information is processed there. By creating an account and using the service you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the safeguards offered by our providers, such as standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

10. Children

RightDesk is for working adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will tell you in the app or by email, and we will update the date at the top of this page.

Help with your account or the app: support@rightdeskreports.com
